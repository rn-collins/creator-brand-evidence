About

Brand decisions are business decisions. They deserve more rigor than taste alone.

Too much brand and creator work is aesthetic, opaque, trend-driven, or disconnected from how businesses actually make decisions. This studio exists to change that—by treating brand and creator strategy as a research, trust, governance, and risk discipline.

Why this approach exists

Evidence, judgment, and honesty about uncertainty.

Most brand work fails because the reasoning is invisible—founder preference, internal politics, or trend imitation dressed up as strategy. The alternative is to make the evidence explicit, grade its strength, name what we don’t know, and test where we can.

That’s especially important in trust-sensitive, regulated, and expert-led categories, where bad messaging, vague claims, or poor disclosure can create real harm—not just weak conversion.

The cross-disciplinary lens

A pan-disciplinary background that maps to this work.

  • Neuroscience & developmental psychology — attention, trust formation, parasociality, identity, habit loops.
  • Compliance & regulatory — claims review, disclosure systems, risk classification, audit trails.
  • Law / future attorney — contracts, IP, endorsement law, FTC, platform terms, brand safety.
  • Product management — workflows, stakeholder management, dashboards, experiment cycles.
  • Research — literature reviews, evidence grading, primary-source synthesis, market mapping.
