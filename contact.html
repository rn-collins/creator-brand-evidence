Before you fill this in

What to have ready, and what happens to it.

A better intake produces a better scoping call. Everything below is optional — the form works with whatever you have today.

Useful to gather first

A link to two or three recent creator posts, your current creator brief or contract template if one exists, and whatever measurement you have on the last campaign — even if it is a screenshot of platform analytics. Gaps in these are themselves a finding, so send what exists rather than waiting to assemble a complete set.

How your materials are handled

Intake submissions and any materials shared during scoping are used to prepare your proposal and audit, and are shared with no one else. Named creators, contract terms and unpublished campaign results stay inside the engagement. If a finding is later written up as a case note, it is anonymised and cleared with you first.

Where this fits best

The audit does its clearest work for brands in trust-sensitive categories — health, legal, wellness, education and regulated consumer goods — that already have creator activity running and want the strategy underneath it made explicit before spend increases. Earlier-stage brands are usually better served by the Partnership Strategy Sprint.

Sizing the engagement first? The services overview sets out each option, and audit deliverables lists exactly what the flagship audit hands back.
