How it's structured

Cadence set to the pace of your creator activity.

Retainers run on a biweekly or monthly cadence depending on how much creator, campaign, and content volume is moving through the brand—more frequent for teams shipping creator content weekly, quarterly-anchored for teams whose main risk is drift between bigger launches. Scope, cadence, and access (async review vs. live working sessions) are set during intake, using the same Evidence Map and Risk Map produced in the Audit as the baseline—so a retainer never starts from zero.

A month on retainer

What the cadence actually looks like.

Retainers go wrong when they become a standing meeting with no artefact. A month on this one produces four things you can point at.

1

Review queue, cleared

Campaign copy, landing pages, creator briefs and posts submitted during the period come back marked against the claims checklist — approved, approved with wording changes, or escalated, each with the reason attached rather than a verdict alone.

2

Decision log entries

Every judgement call gets written down: what was asked, what was decided, and which evidence or rule it rested on. This is what stops the same question being re-litigated next quarter by someone who was not in the room.

3

Evidence Map deltas

The map built during the audit gets updated as claims gain or lose support, competitors change their narrative, and platform or regulatory guidance shifts. You see what moved, not a fresh document each time.

4

A standing risk view

The Risk Map is re-scored against what actually shipped, so exposure is tracked as a trend rather than rediscovered during the next incident. Items that have gone quiet get closed out explicitly.
